## Proposed learner rules

| Area | Proposed Unit 3 rule |
| --- | --- |
| Diagnostic | One completed **20-question** baseline, without pass/fail. Completion unlocks core Practice. DA and DB provide two candidate forms, including one possible technical replacement. |
| Mastery total | **16/20 (80%)** or more, using the unrounded score. Twenty equal-weight single-choice items, one point each; no negative marking. All assigned responses must be submitted. |
| Skill minima | S01, S03, S04, S06, S08 and S09: **at least 1/2** each. S05 conditional: **2/3**. S07 reading detail: **3/4**. All minima and the total must pass. |
| Single-item S02 | Its one natural-feature vocabulary question counts in the total, with **no separate pass veto** (minimum 0/1). S02 still requires Practice readiness. One response is not a reliable separate skill diagnosis. |
| Reporting | Vocabulary **3**, grammar **7**, reading **6**, listening **4**. This is bounded automatic evidence, not complete English competence or CEFR certification. |
| First Mastery readiness | All nine core skills ready: model score **≥0.75 with ≥3 evidence records**, or **≥3 distinct items encountered plus the latest three answers fully correct without hints** within the same release. |
| Practice | **52 core items**, up to **eight per session**. Preserve whole shared-source groups; a session can finish early when eligible supply is exhausted. Unseen-first, then governed repeats. |
| Required Review | **Twelve planned core Practice questions** after the first and second scored failures, with existing completion/help rules and no additional pass percentage. Assessment sources stay out of Review. |
| Attempts | At most **three scored Mastery attempts** using MA/MB/MC, with required Review between failures. Review remains available after the last failure; no fourth attempt. |
| Audio | **Two plays per assessed recording per attempt**, shared by its linked questions; normal speed, no transcript/translation or seeking. Practice/Review replay remains unlimited. |
| Technical interruption | Resume first. A supported persistent audio failure can end unscored while retaining answers and exposure. Replacement requires a complete unused eligible form; exposed forms are not reset. |
| Writing | One **80–100-word** W01 response, four criteria scored **0–3**, separate **/12** feedback. No automatic readiness/pass gate or numerical writing pass threshold; pending teacher review is not zero or failure. |
| Speaking and timing | Speaking stays classroom-assessed. No timer or invented completion-time estimate. |

The model retains alpha=2, beta=2, flat difficulty weight, minimum evidence=3, a 0.65 multiplier for positive hinted evidence and no discount to negative evidence. Four perfect unhinted responses from the prior reach 0.75; the recent-answer route can qualify earlier. Scores below 0.5 indicate support, below 0.75 development, and at least 0.75 security, subject to minimum evidence. Partial Practice scores may update the model; recovery requires whole-item correctness. Model labels do not certify independent language proficiency.

The existing recovery rule counts distinct items across the release history, rather than requiring the latest three responses to concern distinct items. Governed repeated recent answers can therefore qualify after three distinct items have been encountered. Valid answers retained after an unscored technical exit may also contribute. This proposal preserves and discloses those semantics.

## Five prepared forms

DA, DB, MA, MB and MC each contain **20 single-choice items**: ten language items, six reading items and four listening items. Each has two reading sets of three questions and two audio sets of two questions. Presentation keeps all members of a source together. The retained exact-version assembly verifies **100 different question identities, 20 separate assessed source families and ten different assessed audio versions**, with no Practice source-family overlap.

These are prepared bindings, not live qualified or allocated forms. Source-family uniqueness does not prove unseen exposure or equal difficulty. Exact current versions, revocation, compatibility, protection and exposure must still qualify at binding. The private packet holds the version/source identities and snapshot fingerprints; public source contains no prompts, keys or protected scripts.

Three Mastery forms provide at most three scored attempts **only if no form is lost to technical exposure**. Losing one whole form leaves at most two scored attempts; losing two leaves one; losing all three leaves none. There is no extra reserve. A second Diagnostic form is usable only if still eligible. General device failures use saved-state resume; the existing audio exit is not a new general cancellation or appeals system.

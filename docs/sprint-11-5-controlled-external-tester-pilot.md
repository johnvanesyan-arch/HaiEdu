# Sprint 11.5 — Controlled External Tester Pilot

**Status:** planned after Sprint 11 technical handoff readiness.

## Purpose

Allow one or more real users/testers to use the exact current Physics 7 Chapter I release under a controlled operating process and produce evidence that can legitimately inform Sprint 12.

Sprint 11 closes on **technical handoff readiness**. Sprint 11.5 owns the human/testing work that was previously mixed into Sprint 11L.

The existing Sprint 11L pilot-governance tables and fail-closed status guard remain authoritative. Sprint 11 closure does not mark human gates as passed and does not advance the pilot to `entry_ready`.

## Entry requirements

- Sprint 11 technical handoff report approved.
- Operational participation-authorization owner named.
- Participant/guardian information and authorization workflow approved where applicable.
- Tester/cohort scope, dates and teacher scope defined.
- Identity minimization, withdrawal, suspension and deletion handling defined.
- Only authorized testers/participants enrolled.
- Exact target curriculum release and blueprint provenance preserved.

## Controlled tester run

Observe and record:

- Diagnostic → Practice → Mastery lifecycle;
- teacher/usability evidence where applicable;
- repetition/exposure behavior;
- item/content defects;
- standards-alignment observations;
- operational defects and accepted limitations.

## Evidence rule

Informal QA/usability testing may begin after Sprint 11 technical handoff, but that activity does **not** count as formal pilot or Sprint 12 calibration evidence unless the Sprint 11.5 authorization and cohort gates are satisfied.

If a tester is a minor, any applicable participant/guardian authorization remains required for formal pilot evidence; Sprint 11.5 does not bypass that requirement.

## Exit criteria

- Formal pilot/test report approved.
- Evidence sufficiency decision recorded for Sprint 12 calibration.
- If evidence is insufficient, record a no-change/defer disposition rather than inventing calibration.

## ChatGPT execution mode

**Hybrid.**

Use regular chat for authorization rules, evidence sufficiency, interpretation and disposition decisions. Use Work mode for cohort setup, operational verification, evidence collection, repository/database updates and synchronization after the human decisions are complete.

Row · Uses today's roster wrapping rules. The pass result and the support flag are separate cells; the support count is unchanged. The flag cell shows the most recent qualifying red estimate: skill, kind of work, and the date of the latest answer included in it. «Դիտել ևս 1 հմտություն» is a link that opens the panel at box 3 and moves focus there. «ստուգման ավարտից առաջ» appears only if that answer predates the check's completion. Not shown: first-attempt readiness, attempt numbers, retry availability, per-skill check scores. The recent-accuracy column is left out pending your decision.

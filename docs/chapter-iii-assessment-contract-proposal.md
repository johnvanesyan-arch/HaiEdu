## Delivery, readiness and scoring

1. **Diagnostic:** one completed baseline attempt per learner for this chapter, drawn as one whole qualified form from two disjoint 16-question forms. All sixteen responses contribute to per-skill diagnostic feedback; no Diagnostic pass mark. Completion unlocks Practice under the existing platform rule.
2. **Practice:** eight questions per session, drawn from the approved 80-question practice bank under the existing exposure/help rules. All eight skills remain available for learning and recommendations. Bank size is not a requirement to answer every question.
3. **Mastery readiness:** require readiness only for S01, S03, S04 and S06. Preserve the current numerical model: score at least 0.72 with at least three evidence records, **or** the existing recent-recovery route (at least four distinct attempted items and at least four correct, hint-free responses among the latest five attempts). Do not convert this OR into AND or introduce new independence claims. S02/S05/S07/S08 readiness cannot block entry.
4. **Mastery delivery:** sixteen required responses, no hints, at most three attempts using three qualified disjoint forms. The six supporting/additional questions are still answered for feedback; their correctness does not affect passing. This is non-gating scoring, not optional skipping of questions.
5. **Mastery pass:** at least **8/10 mandatory-outcome questions correct**, plus at least one correct in each of S01/S03/S04/S06. The per-skill minimum prevents two errors in a two-question required skill from being hidden by the overall score. Supporting and additional results cannot compensate for a missing mandatory outcome, and cannot make a successful mandatory-outcome result fail.
6. **Results:** show the mandatory-outcome score and pass decision separately from supporting (out of two) and additional (out of four) results. An optional overall 16-question descriptive score must never be labelled the pass score. The three scales and the readiness scope must agree across student, teacher and school views.
7. **Retries:** preserve the existing required chapter-review lifecycle between unsuccessful Mastery attempts and final failure after the third unsuccessful attempt. The proposed Chapter Review length is twelve, with existing hint/help rules. Required recovery targets come from mandatory skills; supporting/additional mistakes may inform recommendations but cannot create a retry lock or alter the mandatory pass.
8. **Laboratory work:** retain the separate teacher-observed practical checklist from preparation. No MCQ result, overall chapter label or automatic badge claims performed laboratory competence. The teacher workflow remains separately scoped.

## Decision and alternatives

**Recommend the structure above** because it follows the mandatory/additional programme distinction, preserves sixteen-question delivery and the 80% threshold, and reports useful supporting evidence without an accidental pass gate. The trade-off is a shared runtime/reporting update and more coarse evidence than a longer formal examination; no empirical reliability or equivalence claim is made.

The simpler alternatives are a core-only Mastery assessment with separate extension activities, or scoring all sixteen. Core-only delivery changes the accepted breadth and bank plan; all-sixteen scoring can make supporting/additional knowledge necessary to pass. Neither alternative is silently adopted.

## Review method

Lexical retrieval ranked the nearest current, historical, retired, guide and misconception records. Ranking was only a search aid. Each candidate was then compared across:

- scenario and representation;
- required solution steps;
- required inference;
- keyed answer and distractor logic;
- explanation and learner guide;
- diagram or stimulus dependency;
- related Chapter I–V skills and exposure history;
- every other candidate in this batch.

Intentional Practice reinforcement is labelled `intentional_practice_variant`; it does not claim independent assessment capacity. Distinct calculations, diagnoses, evidence judgments, syntheses and transfer inferences are labelled `new_reasoning_coverage`. No protected candidate relies on a textbook image.

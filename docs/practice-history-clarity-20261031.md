## Problem and bounded correction

The full three-sentence scope notice repeated in each session row. Both behaviours come from the shared saved-result renderer, also used by the learner’s course/history/player. Supported v2 counts and v3 points stay on their original scales. The existing two-decimal point display is retained; the saved pass decision and pass-role flag remain authoritative.
- Teacher and student histories show individual activity cards with the existing mode, completion/status and exact result. Original version/provenance details remain expandable. History requires no horizontal table scrolling. Enlarged-text phone spacing is capped, and the redundant outer History card is removed at narrow widths; the user’s font size and every result remain intact.
- Each history section has one keyboard-operable scope disclosure. Full rules and standalone player results keep their original scope text. Teacher Diagnostic/Mastery summary cards share a disclosure and no longer nest block results inside a bold inline wrapper.
- The same group renderer serves school/master aggregates without combining contracts, computing new percentages or changing their original pass counts.

**Maya:** Which music do you want for our class video?

**Leo:** There are two versions of the same song. The first starts with loud drums and an electric guitar. It's quite fast.

**Maya:** That sounds good for the basketball scenes. What does the second version sound like?

**Leo:** It's slower. There's a piano and a guitar, but no drums. The singer's voice is clearer too.

**Maya:** I prefer that version for listening at home. But our video starts with a basketball game, so the faster one could work better.

**Leo:** What about the next part, when we explain our art project? People need to hear us speaking.

**Maya:** We can turn the music down for that part.

**Leo:** So, the first version for the opening, then lower the volume when we speak?

**Maya:** Yes. Let's try it and check that every sentence is clear.

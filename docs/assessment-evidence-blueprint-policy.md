# Prospective Assessment Evidence-Blueprint Policy

**Policy version:** `haiedu-evidence-blueprint-v1`  
**Adoption date:** 2026-10-02  
**Scope:** all new item templates and question families created after adoption  
**Legacy treatment:** existing templates, items, versions, approvals and learner evidence are unchanged

**Whole-bank review addendum:** `haiedu-whole-skill-review-v1`, approved 2026-10-06. Applies to **every future new or revised question batch**, including additions to legacy families, across all subjects, grades, chapters, releases, schools, classes and teachers, and Diagnostic, Practice, Chapter Review and Mastery Check banks. The prospective database profile below remains v1; this wider authoring requirement is not limited to profiled/new templates.

## Mandatory whole-bank review before deployment

1. **Freeze the comparison corpus.** Read the complete applicable skill bank, not just the preceding batch or current template: drafts, reviewed/published items, retired items, relevant immutable revisions and exposed history across releases and all assessment purposes. Check related/equivalent skill/outcome mappings so another code does not hide a repeated task. Record item codes/versions, snapshot time, source revision and a corpus fingerprint or equivalent version guard. State absent banks explicitly; inaccessible protected content is an unresolved gate, not permission to omit it.
2. **Compare substance.** For each candidate compare the scenario, data/diagram or textbook figure, response demand, correct solution steps and required inference, distractor reasoning and teaching goal against that corpus and the other candidates. Changing numbers, names, phrasing, answer order, artwork or evidence-purpose labels does not establish new coverage. Reusing a figure is not automatically duplication; using a different figure is not automatically novelty.
3. **Record a disposition.** Classify each candidate as **new reasoning coverage**, **intentional practice variant**, or **redundant—rework/omit**. List the nearest existing items and explain the actual additional demand or specific practice benefit. Similarity flags require educational judgment; neither identical-text checks nor purpose/family fingerprints establish semantic distinctness. Do not count a practice variant as a new learning outcome or an independent new reasoning path.
4. **Verify source fidelity.** Cite textbook edition, chapter/section and pages/figures, plus the applicable programme outcome. Verify every premise, correct answer, distractor explanation and solution step. Clearly identify authored applications/analogies and their assumptions. Creativity stays within what is taught; it cannot manufacture unintroduced facts, laws, procedures or ambiguous exceptions to reach a target. Use the illustration policy and an explicit no-image rationale for verbal tasks. Do not attach an inaccurate misconception merely to satisfy mapping QA; new definitions remain subject to human approval.
5. **Protect assessment validity.** Compare shared practice against protected diagnostic/mastery banks without exposing protected prompts in public reports or learner material. A new ID or different wording does not make an equivalent scenario/solution safe for protected use. Record restricted collision references, exposure/separation decisions and unresolved risks through authorized evidence. Existing pool/form separation and exposure gates remain mandatory.
6. **Use targets honestly.** Twelve per skill and other bank counts are planning targets, never instructions to pad. A useful variant may support practice, but its contribution must be explicit. If the source/skill cannot support enough defensible items, stop that expansion, report the quality-approved inventory and shortfall, and obtain John's decision on the bank plan. Do not silently reduce assessment lengths, reuse protected items, loosen readiness or pass release with an undersized bank. Existing delivery/scoring contracts remain unchanged until separately authorized.
7. **Close the handoff.** Commit the comparison matrix and source rationale with the batch, identify the author/reviewer and decision date, and re-read the corpus immediately before deployment. Changes to a candidate, its source, or relevant bank content invalidate the affected comparison and require reassessment. Human question/guide/misconception approval and SME/release gates remain separate. Substantive corrections produce new versions; never overwrite approved historical content or manufacture approvals.

The predeployment evidence record must contain: scope and complete version inventory; source locators; per-item scenario/solution/inference profiles; nearest matches; disposition and rationale; illustration reuse; protected-bank findings; target versus defensible coverage/shortfall; version/fingerprint guard; open reviews; and the author/reviewer identity or an explicit automated-author provenance. Use [the S02 audit](chapter-ii-s02-whole-bank-review.md) as an example, not a subject-specific default.

**Enforcement boundary:** this addendum is a mandatory platform-wide authoring and deployment-review gate, also recorded in `AGENTS.md`. It does not claim that the current database or QA endpoint automatically recognizes repeated scenarios or solution paths. Their structural/profile checks remain necessary but insufficient. No semantic detector, retrospective automatic rejection, learner change or universal historical-bank audit is claimed by adoption.

## Natural Armenian and source language — 2026-10-12

Use natural, age-appropriate Armenian in learner-facing questions, options, explanations and guides. Consult the applicable approved textbook/programme for established terminology and subject facts. Check language and subject meaning separately: avoid literal translations, mixed-script words, unnecessary qualifiers and reading complexity that adds no useful evidence. Explicit model assumptions must stay clear. Preserve approved wording when no correction is needed; substantive corrections still require a new version and fresh review.

John explicitly reaffirmed this authoring requirement when authorizing the S05-M05 v2 comparison refresh. It applies to Armenian learner-facing content across subjects, without adding language or subject assumptions to the shared runtime. Textbook wording is a reference for natural terminology and accurate concepts; authored applications must still state their assumptions and pass source, semantic and human review.

No schema, runtime, automatic semantic detector, existing approval or learner evidence is changed.

The additional disposition is **protected parallel assessment variant** (`protected_parallel_assessment_variant`). It may assess the same taught claim/procedure as another task when a substantively new stimulus requires fresh interpretation and recognition of an exposed worked example does not supply the complete answer. It adds candidate bank capacity, not a learning outcome or an independent reasoning path. A number, name, phrase, answer order, artwork or ID change alone does not qualify.

Every such candidate must record:

1. The authorized manifest slot, repeated taught claim/procedure, specific new stimulus interpretation, textbook/programme locators, explicit assumptions and illustration or no-image rationale.
2. A frozen full applicable current/history bank and related/equivalent mappings, plus all within-batch pairs; nearest practice/guide and protected matches; complete solution and distractor comparisons. Inaccessible or stale evidence remains a blocking gap.
3. A protection judgment addressing the actual complete scenario/solution, not merely different IDs. Do not reclassify existing practice into protected banks or publish the protected solution in learner guides. Item/stimulus separation, per-learner exposure controls and disjoint-form planning remain mandatory.
4. Source-supported science and procedures only. Explicit premises may delimit a taught model; they may not introduce an untaught law, calculation or probability model to fill a slot.
5. Ordinary current-version/family QA and explicit human content/definition review. SME/exposure review is required before allocation or release. Parallelism, difficulty, reliability and equivalence remain provisional until suitable pilot evidence exists.
6. Actual qualified/staged/reviewed counts, rejected or reworked candidates and remaining gaps after the pilot. Further use of this disposition requires the owner's next decision; numerical completion is authorized only when the owner explicitly includes those remaining slots, and does not waive quality; previously omitted proposals are never automatically reinstated.

## Purpose

Question-family planning must describe what evidence each item contributes before the family can pass technical QA. This prevents a nominally varied set of questions from repeatedly testing the same recall operation while preserving the existing authoring, review, versioning, approval, release and learner-runtime architecture.

The policy is subject-neutral. A subject extension may define suitable stimulus, response formats, scoring and misconceptions, but it may not replace the shared evidence-purpose vocabulary or bypass the existing governance gates.

## Shared evidence purposes

| Machine value | Authoring meaning | Natural Armenian label |
|---|---|---|
| `core_understanding` | Identify or explain the central concept | Հիմնական ըմբռնում |
| `application` | Use the concept in a represented, practical or observed situation | Կիրառում |
| `misconception_diagnosis` | Distinguish the concept from a plausible misconception | Թյուր պատկերացման բացահայտում |
| `transfer_reasoning` | Apply or explain the concept in a changed context | Տեղափոխում և հիմնավորում |

These values classify evidence purpose, not item type. A multiple-choice, true/false, numeric, quantity or future governed response type may serve any appropriate purpose.

## Prospective authoring contract

Every new item template must declare:

1. `authoringProfileVersion = haiedu-evidence-blueprint-v1`;
2. `evidenceBlueprintPolicy = distinct-purpose-v1`;
3. one or more `requiredEvidencePurposes` selected from the shared vocabulary;
4. an outcome-appropriate family size rather than a platform-wide fixed item count.

Every non-retired item attached to that template must declare one valid `evidencePurpose`. Technical QA evaluates the whole active family and fails when a required purpose is absent, an item lacks a purpose, or an unknown purpose is used.

Every QA result for a profiled family stores a deterministic family-state fingerprint containing the template version and evidence requirements plus active membership, sibling item versions and evidence-purpose assignments. Approval re-evaluates the current active family and compares that fingerprint instead of trusting only a stored per-item pass. The comparison, review record and approval update run in one database transaction while family and template mutations take the same transaction-scoped lock. If a sibling or template was retired, restored, revised or reassigned after that QA run, approval fails closed and requires QA to be run again even when the remaining family still covers every required purpose.

Templates may set `enforceUniqueEvidencePurposes = true` when each required purpose must occur exactly once. This is appropriate for the four-item Sprint 15B.8 family. Larger or otherwise justified families may repeat a purpose; they still must cover every declared required purpose.

## What does not change

- Existing question families are not backfilled, rewritten or re-approved solely because of this policy.
- The policy does not publish content, create an assessment pool or blueprint, or make an item learner-visible.
- Existing semantic fingerprints, immutable item versions, misconception mappings, current-version QA, human approval, SME gates and curriculum-release gates remain authoritative.
- The shared server-side answer-option shuffle remains the delivery safeguard; evidence purpose does not determine answer position.
- Subject-aware content decisions remain explicit extensions built on the shared contract.

## Enforcement layers

The database rejects new templates that omit the prospective profile and rejects items attached to profiled templates when their authoring profile or evidence purpose is missing or invalid. The protected content-quality function then performs family-level coverage checks and records the evidence blueprint in the QA result.

This two-layer design prevents malformed writes while keeping contextual family-quality judgments in the governed QA process.

## Adoption test

The policy is considered adopted when:

1. legacy templates remain valid without modification;
2. a new unprofiled template is rejected;
3. an item with no valid evidence purpose is rejected for a profiled template;
4. QA reports the required purposes, actual counts and any missing/invalid entries;
5. approval re-checks the current active family and rejects stale passing QA after a family-membership change;
6. the first adopted family remains learner-inactive until the ordinary review and release gates are completed.

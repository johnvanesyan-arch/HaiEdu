## Recommended assessment contract

Use **Diagnostic 12, Practice 8, Mastery 12 and Chapter Review 12**. Diagnostic has one completed baseline and no passing score. Mastery allows at most three attempts and passes with **10/12 correct plus at least one correct answer in each of eight required skills**. The policy threshold is 80%; with twelve questions this requires ten correct (83⅓%). Required Chapter Review follows failed attempts one and two. Existing final-failure behavior remains after attempt three. Diagnostic and Mastery have no hints.

Optional S09/S10 remain available in Practice/Review and separate reporting; they do not block Diagnostic completion, readiness or passing. Their authored budget contains no Diagnostic/Mastery candidates. Readiness applies only to S01–S08 and preserves the existing model: secure score ≥0.72 with at least three evidence records **OR** recent recovery through four distinct attempted items and four hint-free correct responses among the latest five. No numerical model tuning is proposed. Practice/Review retain their existing help and exposure rules.

**Trade-off:** S01, S02, S04 and S07 each have only one question per form. Missing any one fails the skill minimum, even with 11/12 overall. This is a short provisional knowledge check, not a validated certification. A 16-question Mastery with two per required skill needs **at least eight additional Mastery candidates**, specifically two each in those four skills, for three disjoint forms. Two 16-question Diagnostic forms likewise need at least eight additional Diagnostic candidates in those skills. These are numerical minima, not a claim that eight defensible independent candidates can be authored. The present recommendation retains the accepted bank without padding it.

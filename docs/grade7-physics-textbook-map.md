## First MVP candidate

**Chapter I** is the leading candidate for the first MVP curriculum slice because it already contains several capabilities that are central to the platform architecture:

- identifying and classifying physical phenomena;
- observation versus experiment;
- hypothesis / evidence / conclusion reasoning;
- physical quantities and SI units;
- measurement instruments;
- scale division value;
- measurement error;
- direct and indirect measurement;
- practical/laboratory measurement tasks.

It therefore supports Knowledge → Application → Transfer and evidence/reasoning tasks without requiring the platform to begin with advanced formulas.

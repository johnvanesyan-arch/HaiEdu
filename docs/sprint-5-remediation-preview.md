# Sprint 5 Preview — Adaptive Practice + Remediation Engine

Sprint 4 identifies where the learner needs support. Sprint 5 will determine how the platform actively teaches that gap.

Planned capabilities:
- misconception codes;
- progressive hints;
- worked examples with different numbers;
- easier bridge questions;
- prerequisite repair;
- limited retry rules;
- no pointless identical-question loops;
- mastery evidence only after independent responses.

This is intentionally separated from Sprint 4 so diagnostic interpretation and remediation behaviour remain distinct product layers.

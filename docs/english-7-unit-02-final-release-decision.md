The recent-three rule can reuse previously encountered items after the distinct-item requirement is met. Core listening has limited evidence; Diagnostic evidence and governed repeats are explicitly accepted, not represented as fresh independent coverage.

After a failed first or second Mastery attempt, a twelve-question Review is required. There are at most three scored attempts and three forms, with no extra reserve. There is no invented reset, recycled replacement or automatic failure solely for exhausted capacity. Assessed listening permits two plays per shared recording at normal speed without a transcript; Practice/Review replay is unlimited. There is no assessment timer. Writing feedback does not change automatic mastery/readiness.

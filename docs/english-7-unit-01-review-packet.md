## Activity 1 · book p.4 · core practice

Describe behaviour with neutral vocabulary. Do not classify people as morally positive or negative.

**Teaching guide G01: Describe without judging.** A word can describe behaviour, such as patient, or appearance, such as curly-haired. Neither appearance nor a clothing style tells us everything about a person. Choose a word from the evidence in the sentence, not from a stereotype.

**Worked practice example:** Someone who says “please” and thanks a helper is being polite.

## Activity 5 · book p.6 · core practice

Teach the style meanings in the guide before matching.

**Teaching guide G03: Style words.** Casual means relaxed and informal; classic describes an established lasting style; retro is inspired by the past; trendy is popular now; chic means elegant and stylish; flashy is very noticeable. Conservative suggests traditional choices; eccentric suggests unusual choices; sloppy means not neat; old-fashioned means no longer modern. These labels describe a look and are not judgments about a person’s worth.

**Worked practice example:** A look inspired by clothes from an earlier decade can be called retro.

## Activity 13 · book p.12 · core practice

Teach adjective/adverb function rather than an absolute word-position rule.

**Teaching guide G05: Describe a thing, an action or a feeling.** An adjective can describe a noun after a linking verb: The tea smells pleasant. A person can also be the cause.

**Worked practice example:** An exciting story makes a reader excited.

## Activity 15 · book p.14 · core practice

Use too/enough, count/mass quantifiers and the taught positions.

**Teaching guide G06: Enough, too and comparisons.** Enough shows sufficiency: enough time; warm enough. Too shows excess for a stated purpose: too cold to sit outside. Use too many with countable plurals and too much with an amount. Compare two things or actions with a comparative; use a superlative for the extreme in a stated group. Much and far can strengthen a comparative.

**Worked practice example:** The room is warm enough to work in. This route is much shorter than that one.

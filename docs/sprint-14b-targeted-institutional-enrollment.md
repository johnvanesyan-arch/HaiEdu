# Sprint 14B — Targeted institutional enrollment foundation

**Status:** Invitation, membership, bootstrap and staff-account provisioning foundations deployed through 2026-09-24. The user reports a school admin and teacher account activated after an invitation resend and SMTP setup. This recipient report supersedes the earlier zero-school snapshot below; authenticated production verification and the two-school tenant release remain gated. Shared sign-in, standalone student self-registration and an institutional-teacher guard against legacy schoolless classes are deployed; school-scoped learner entitlement is not yet connected.

## Flow

1. A school admin with an active school membership can issue a targeted invitation for an existing active student or teacher account. A teacher can issue a student class invitation only for an active assignment in that school. The recipient can be identified by account UUID or existing-account email; email lookup is service-only and no email copy is stored in the public invitation table.
2. The server generates a 256-bit random token. Only its SHA-256 digest is stored. The one-time plaintext token is returned to the issuer. Invitations expire within seven days (default 48 hours), name one recipient and role, and may be revoked before use.
3. The recipient signs in with the named account. Redemption locks the invitation row, revalidates the issuer, class, year and recipient, then creates or reuses an active institutional membership and, for a class invitation, creates a separate time-aware institutional class enrollment. Consumption and enrollment are one transaction.
4. These enrollments do not enter legacy `class_memberships`. Migration 0082 still blocks that table for institution-scoped classes, so the current Physics 7 learner runtime cannot treat this new membership as course entitlement.

## Authorization boundaries

- `service_issue_institutional_invitation_14b`, `service_redeem_institutional_invitation_14b`, `service_revoke_institutional_invitation_14b`, and `service_lookup_invitee_by_email_14b` are executable only by `service_role`. The Edge Functions authenticate a live session, and the database functions enforce the school and assignment boundary.
- `institutional_invitations` and `institutional_class_enrollments` have RLS and no ordinary client grants/policies. Even `service_role` has no direct INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE on these two tables; their mutations occur through the controlled database functions. Invitation and enrollment mutations enter the protected academic identity audit log.
- School A actors cannot issue for School B through the DB function. A copied token cannot be redeemed by a different account. Concurrent attempts serialize on the invitation row; consumed and revoked tokens cannot be reused.
- School registration tokens for new accounts, school-admin delegation, teacher/class UI, and institutional course entitlement remain outside this bounded slice. Membership and enrollment termination use migrations 0086–0087 and a separate authenticated endpoint. First-admin bootstrap is now available for an existing eligible account through a platform-admin-only endpoint.

## Staff account issuance and recovery checkpoint (2026-09-24)

- Migrations [0093–0100](https://github.com/johnvanesyan-arch/armenian-mastery-platform/tree/main/supabase/migrations) add a seven-day, recipient-bound staff invitation ledger, active teacher subject grants, assignment enforcement and a complete learner-history guard before teacher role conversion. An existing learner with learning, pilot or institutional history cannot be silently repurposed as staff. Direct client writes to the ledger and subject grants are denied; service-role RPCs recheck the issuer and recipient during activation.
- A live platform **admin** initiates the first school's name/code and first school-admin email from [the account console](https://github.com/johnvanesyan-arch/armenian-mastery-platform/blob/main/web/school-admin.html). After activation, that school's active **school_admin** invites a teacher to their own school and a published subject. The teacher can receive further subject grants or revocation through the guarded management endpoint; a revoked subject ends affected active assignments. Student account/enrollment invitation remains a separate targeted workflow, not a side effect of staff signup.
- [`provision-staff-account`](https://github.com/johnvanesyan-arch/armenian-mastery-platform/blob/main/supabase/functions/provision-staff-account/index.ts) sends a Supabase Auth email invitation. Only the named recipient can confirm the link, set a personal password on [account access](https://github.com/johnvanesyan-arch/armenian-mastery-platform/blob/main/web/account-access.html), and activate the institutional role in one database transaction through `accept-staff-account`. Admins do not choose, receive or view passwords. Ordinary sign-in uses email and password; the role routes to its authorized portal. The access page offers `resetPasswordForEmail`; the email link leads back to the password form and `updateUser`. The reset request reports a delivery failure without revealing whether the address exists. Passwords are never recovered in plaintext.
- If Auth email invitation succeeds but ledger registration fails, the recipient may have an unprivileged Auth account; the endpoint reports failure and logs it for explicit reconciliation. It never deletes the returned Auth identity. Invitations expire after seven days and activation rechecks the issuer; invitation reissue/revocation UI and real email-delivery reconciliation are operational follow-up gates.
- The [rollback probe](https://github.com/johnvanesyan-arch/armenian-mastery-platform/blob/main/tests/sprint-14b-staff-provisioning-probe.sql) exercised wrong-recipient denial, first-school/teacher activation, issuer audit, teacher subject grant and revocation/assignment cascade, then removed all fixture data. Focused endpoint tests passed 3/3; syntax checks passed. Public `/account-access` and unauthenticated `/school-admin` pages rendered on production. No live invitation email or password-reset link was sent to a real account in this checkpoint, so deliverability, Auth redirect allow-list and authenticated recipient browser activation remain unverified.

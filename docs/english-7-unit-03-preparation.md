## Factual quality references

- [USDA organic standards](https://www.ams.usda.gov/grades-standards/organic-standards) and [allowed/prohibited substances](https://www.ams.usda.gov/rules-regulations/organic/national-list): organic production is governed by specified practices and permitted inputs, not an absence of all chemicals. This is an editorial counterexample to the textbook definition, not Armenian certification advice.
- [IPCC AR6 synthesis, A.1](https://report.ipcc.ch/ar6syr/headline.html): recent global warming is caused by human activities. Replace the false balance in activity 18; pupils may debate responses and trade-offs without being asked to endorse inaccurate premises.
- [UNESCO Rio landscape description](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1100/): use supported mountain/coast/Guanabara Bay description. Omit the unsupported claim that this bay contains more water than every other bay. The final natural-wonder fact cards need their own primary-source checks; this preparation does not certify facts that have not yet been written.

Unqualified rainforest percentages, universal claims and time-dependent loss rates are removed or held for exact primary-source verification rather than retained as quiz facts. No new scientific expertise is a prerequisite for an English question.

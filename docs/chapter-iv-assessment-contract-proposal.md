## Recommendation

Use **19 Diagnostic questions** and **15 Mastery questions**. Keep one completed Diagnostic, three possible Mastery attempts, Practice 8 and required Chapter Review 12 between failed attempts. Mastery passes at **12/15 (80%) plus at least one correct answer in every required skill**. No hint is available in Diagnostic/Mastery; existing Practice/Review help and exposure rules remain unchanged.

This supports the existing three-attempt lifecycle within the accepted 236-item bank, separates supporting/additional content from compulsory passing, and avoids adding question identities merely to fill forms. Five targeted revisions are proposed below. The 236-question bank is not a student session length.

Diagnostic samples all 14 skills and has no passing score. Completion unlocks Practice. The three supporting skills and S14 remain taught, available in Practice/Review and visible in Diagnostic feedback; they cannot block Mastery readiness or passing. Mastery does not assess them separately. Individual form coverage represents sampled knowledge/interpretation of M1–M11, not every subclause on every form.

Readiness applies to the ten required skills, using the unchanged model: secure score at least 0.72 with at least three evidence records **OR** the existing recent-recovery route (four distinct attempted items and four correct hint-free responses among the latest five). Do not change OR to AND. The existing third-unsuccessful-attempt final-failure rule remains.

**Trade-off:** S01/S02/S04/S06/S07 have only one question per form. A wrong answer to any of those fails the per-skill minimum, including at 14/15 overall. The proposal is a short provisional knowledge check, not an empirically validated certification. A 20-question Mastery with two per required skill would reduce this single-question dependence but needs at least ten additional mandatory Mastery slots numerically, plus direct-coverage/protection work. A 19-question all-skill Mastery would instead require at least three additional supporting/additional slots for three disjoint forms without improving required-skill precision. Neither expansion is silently authorized.

## Practical evidence and MVP scope

**Recommend a separately authorized knowledge-only provisional pilot**, because the current platform does not yet record and verify performed experiments. M3 density measurement and M8 experimental demonstration of Hooke’s law remain unassessed as practical performances. Correct MCQs may show planning or interpretation; no chapter result, badge or teacher report may certify that a student performed the experiments.

The proposed policy reporting label is «Գիտելիքներ․ փորձերի կատարումը չի գնահատվում». Natural learner instructions should state: «Ստուգարքը բաղկացած է 15 հարցից։ Հանձնելու համար պետք է ճիշտ պատասխանել առնվազն 12 հարցի և յուրաքանչյուր պարտադիր հմտությունից ունենալ առնվազն մեկ ճիշտ պատասխան։ Այս ստուգարքը չի հաստատում, որ կատարել եք խտության որոշման և Հուկի օրենքի ստուգման փորձերը»։ These are proposal texts, not deployed UI.

The separate future practical workflow must record the student, school/year/class/release, task/rubric version, raw observations, calculations/table or graph, teacher identity/date and a disposition for each criterion. Reuse the textbook-based five-criterion density and Hooke drafts in [preparation](chapter-iv-preparation.md#practical-evidence-drafts). Missing evidence stays unassessed; no MCQ score creates a practical approval. Teacher evidence remains scoped to authorized school/class assignments, with immutable review history. S12 friction work supports M10; it is not a new mandatory outcome.

The alternative is to build and verify that governed practical workflow before activation. It offers fuller outcome evidence and takes more implementation time. The knowledge-only option needs an explicit Chapter IV decision; earlier chapter exceptions do not transfer automatically. Specialist review and final exposure qualification likewise require a Chapter-IV-specific provisional disposition before any pilot activation.

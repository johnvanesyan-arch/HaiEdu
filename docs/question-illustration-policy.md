# Platform-wide question illustration requirement

Approved by John: 2026-10-05. Applies to question authoring and finalization across every subject, grade, chapter and curriculum release, including Diagnostic, Practice, Chapter Review and Mastery Check. The central question bank follows the same requirement wherever schools, teachers or classrooms use it.

Include relevant illustrations from the applicable source textbook wherever they support the question's task. Preserve scientific meaning and necessary labels; record the textbook edition, page and figure. Include the image in the governed question/version review rather than publishing an unreviewed attachment separately.

Content QA must check desktop/mobile readability, appropriate Armenian alternative text and whether the crop, labels or captions reveal the answer. Protected diagnostic/mastery content must also retain its exposure and separation rules when a textbook stimulus is reused. An illustration is required when it materially supports or is necessary to interpret the task; a purely verbal question need not receive an unrelated picture.

Any scope exception must be explained to John for a decision. This document records the authoring requirement; it does not claim that existing images were audited, that media infrastructure is complete, or that any question or curriculum release has been published.

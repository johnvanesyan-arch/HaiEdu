## READ-HISTORY — Clothing through time

**Purpose:** practice · **Words:** 77 · **Source version:** 1

**Learner label:** Reading source P-A

**Source:** English 7, p.16, activity 18

**Origin:** Condensed textbook adaptation

**Verification:** The three periods and the manufacturing/availability relationship agree with the supplied textbook. Questions ask only about the supplied text.

**Reuse record:** Owner accepts the exact condensed textbook adaptation for this bounded MVP registration. Textbook/page provenance is retained; no publisher licence or public-distribution permission is claimed.

**Exposure:** Familiar Practice source. R01/R02/R03 share one passage; they are not three independent reading samples.

### Passage

Clothing has changed over time. In ancient societies such as Egypt, Greece and Rome, clothes could show a person’s social position.

During the Industrial Revolution, new manufacturing methods made clothing more widely available. This changed how clothes were produced and bought.

Today, clothes can still express personal choices and cultural traditions. Some designers also think about reducing waste and the environmental effects of making clothes. The history of clothing includes changes in materials, production and people’s choices.

## READ-STYLE — Fashion and personal style

**Purpose:** practice · **Words:** 115 · **Source version:** 1

**Learner label:** Reading source P-B

**Source:** English 7, pp.17–18, activity 19

**Origin:** Textbook-based explanation with an original example

**Verification:** Retains the trend/personal-choice distinction and classic/retro/casual meanings. Removes universal and gender-based claims; Ani is an authored example.

**Reuse record:** Owner accepts the exact textbook-based explanation and original Ani example for this bounded MVP registration. No publisher licence or public-distribution permission is claimed.

**Exposure:** Familiar Practice source. G03 and P03/P04 already teach these labels; R06 is intentional reinforcement, not new reasoning coverage.

### Passage

Fashion describes trends that are popular at a particular time. Personal style is the way someone chooses to dress. A person may use a trend, adapt it or decide not to follow it.

Ani notices that bright jackets are popular this month. She keeps wearing her plain shirt because it is comfortable and she likes combining it with different trousers. This is one example of a personal choice.

Classic styles use simple designs that remain popular for a long time. Retro styles draw on clothes from an earlier period. Casual clothes are relaxed choices for everyday activities. These labels describe clothing, not the whole person wearing it. An outfit alone cannot tell us someone’s character.
